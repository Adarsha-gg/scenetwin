# SceneTwin cursor session — 2026-05-27

## What we learned about TRIBE

TRIBE v2 maps video, audio, and text into predicted cortical responses (~20k vertices).
SceneTwin uses the **audio-vs-audiovisual gap** as a **pre-scoring risk forecaster**, not
as a replacement for CLIP or ADQA.

| Signal | Value | Meaning |
|--------|-------|---------|
| Recall@2 | 100% | Both ADQA failure clips ranked top-2 by TRIBE |
| p (hypergeom) | 0.0065 | Unlikely to be chance |
| ρ (TRIBE vs judges) | −0.75 | High visual need → judges disagree on tier order |
| Ensemble ρ | 0.929 | CLIP + frame-grounded ADQA headline (bootstrap CI 0.88–0.96) |
