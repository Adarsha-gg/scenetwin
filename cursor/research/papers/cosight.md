# CoSight

Source: `sources/arxiv_2508.08582.pdf`
arXiv: 2508.08582
Date read: 2026-05-27

## Why they did it

Professional audio description is high quality but does not scale to the volume
of online video. Meanwhile, sighted viewers already comment on visual moments,
but ordinary comments are often vague, subjective, or not anchored to time.

CoSight explores whether lightweight interface nudges can turn normal commenting
into a complementary accessibility resource.

## System

CoSight is a Chrome extension for YouTube. It uses Fogg's Behavior Model to
provide:

- indicators of accessibility gaps
- pop-up hints about what to describe
- reminders to clarify vague comments
- captions/comments as references
- an accessibility mode where BLV viewers can listen to time-anchored comments

## Evidence

In the exploratory study:

- 48 sighted participants wrote 257 comments across 8 videos.
- 94% of comments contained timestamps.
- 89% contained concrete visual details.
- 75% of concrete-detail comments combined personal reactions with descriptive
  information.
- Follow-up interviews included 4 BLV viewers and 4 professional AD writers.

BLV viewers valued grounded descriptive comments, especially when they conveyed
visual context and emotional nuance. Professional AD writers warned that comments
do not match professional rigor and need quality control.

## First-principles challenge

This paper breaks the "accessibility content must be generated by experts or
models" assumption. Some access value can come from socially situated viewer
attention.

But comments are not AD. They are noisy, biased, subjective, redundant, and
sometimes harmful. Their value is as a community evidence layer, not as the
authoritative description track.

## What I would do if I were them

Add a comment quality pipeline:

- grounded visual detail
- timestamp alignment
- subjectivity flag
- redundancy clustering
- BLV usefulness votes
- harm/bias filter

Then let BLV users decide whether they want professional AD, AI summary, or
community context.
